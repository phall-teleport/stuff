Grab a coffee and watch wild sea otters and sea lions lounge along the Coast Guard Pier and Cannery Row.

Free

Head out onto the Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuary to spot humpbacks, orcas, and migrating gray whales.

Seasonal

Scenic

Tide Pool & Ocean Tips

🕒 Check the Tide Chart

Low tide reveals the best tide pools — anemones, sea stars, and hermit crabs — especially around Asilomar and Pacific Grove.

🧥 Layer Up

Monterey stays cool and foggy year-round. Bring a windbreaker even in summer — the bay breeze doesn't care about the season.

🐾 Give Wildlife Space

Otters, seals, and shorebirds are protected in the sanctuary. Admire from a distance and never feed wild animals.
