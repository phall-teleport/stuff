🌆 Fun Things to Do in Seattle

Your guide to the Emerald City — coffee, mountains, sound, and skyline included.

🛰️

Ride to the Top of the Space Needle

Take the elevator up 520 feet for a 360° view of downtown, Puget Sound, and Mount Rainier on a clear day.

🐟

Explore Pike Place Market

Watch the famous fish-throwing fishmongers, grab a bite, and browse local crafts at this century-old market.

☕

Visit the Original Starbucks

Steps from Pike Place, sip a coffee where it all began back in 1971.

🎨

Marvel at Chihuly Garden and Glass

Wander through vibrant blown-glass sculptures and a lush garden right at the base of the Space Needle.

⛴️

Take a Ferry Across Puget Sound

Hop on a Washington State Ferry to Bainbridge Island for skyline views and a laid-back day trip.

🎸

Rock Out at MoPOP

The Museum of Pop Culture celebrates music, sci-fi, and gaming with interactive exhibits for all ages.

🌲

Hike Discovery Park

Escape the city in Seattle's largest park, with forest trails, bluffs, and a lighthouse on the water.

🏙️

Stroll the Waterfront & Great Wheel

Walk the newly revamped waterfront and take a spin on the Seattle Great Wheel for sound-side views.

🦑

Visit the Seattle Aquarium

Get up close with giant Pacific octopuses, otters, and local marine life right on the waterfront.

🏀

Catch a Game or Show

Cheer on the Kraken, Storm, or Sounders, or catch a concert at Climate Pledge Arena or T-Mobile Park.

🍜

Eat Your Way Through the International District

Sample dim sum, ramen, and pastries in one of the city's most flavorful neighborhoods.

🚡

Day Trip to Mount Rainier

Just a couple hours away, wildflower meadows and glacier views make for an unforgettable escape.
